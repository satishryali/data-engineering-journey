## Existing Tools workflow

- Select a technology in the existing Tools page and use `/problem`, `/hint`, `/stuck`, `/solved`, `/next`, `/review`, `/progress` and `/why`. The published challenge browser provides optional breadth; prerequisite checks prevent jumps and transitive gaps.
- Each published problem has a stable ID, business situation, specific objective, input/environment, observable expected result, constraints, acceptance criteria, level, effort, prerequisites, three progressive hints and pipeline relevance. The primary allocated tracks have published problems through Level 10. The other retained tracks keep their starter and planned stages.
- `/solved` preserves approach, help sources, code/artifacts and actual results. Failed attempts never erase prior verified work. Hints and known help prevent an independent score. A later independent changed-input attempt requires exact input and expected result and separate verification.
- Per-technology and overall denominators come from the actual published bank. Verified completion and independent evidence are distinct. The existing SQLite journal and canonical checkpoint bridge preserve them through restarts and deliberate cross-computer handoff; draft answers still require journal export.
- The curated 209-problem Job-Ready pathway has four evidence-based assessments dated November 15, December 6, December 27 and January 20. Its eligibility uses demonstrated prerequisites; finishing 4,213 problems is not required. Assessment results do not guarantee employment.

The form records a human report, not automatic execution; a learner success claim alone does not verify a problem. No contained Python runner, cloud execution or AI correctness grading was claimed.
- Production/cloud tasks are bounded synthetic training challenges. They require authorized training environments and redacted run artifacts; unavailable access is `environment_blocked`, not a verified completion. Many advanced tasks are specification and investigation exercises rather than pre-executed labs.
