# RDS-001 - Product Catalogue

## Business Situation

Our fictional Indian retail company is opening its first store. The store needs a product catalogue before normal sales operations can begin.

## Business Requirement

The store manager needs 1,000 products recorded in the operational system. Each product has a unique identifier, product name, product category and selling price in INR.

## Why It Matters

Store staff need to find each product and its selling price before recording sales.

## Available Inputs

The business fields and acceptance criteria below. PostgreSQL is available per learner report; the task connection is not yet verified. No product file, catalogue schema or generator is prescribed or supplied. Use synthetic or properly licensed public data; decide your approach.

## Expected Output

A catalogue of exactly 1,000 products stored in PostgreSQL, retrievable and verifiable with SQL. Supply your implementation and actual verification output without credentials.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Exactly 1,000 initial product records are stored.
2. Product identifiers are unique.
3. Product names are populated (not empty or whitespace-only).
4. Categories are populated (not empty or whitespace-only).
5. Selling prices in INR are positive; store and retrieve their monetary values accurately.
6. Records can be retrieved and verified using SQL.

Use an isolated development target and preserve unrelated data. Keep credentials out of committed code and shared output.
